PILLAR 1, OPERATIONAL FRAGILITY AS COMMERCIAL RISK
Type: Audience
─────────────────────────────────────────
Why this pillar: The ICP's own world. A COO, CFO or CEO of a founder-led
$10M-$100M firm carries five compounding risks (governance, data integrity,
key-person, friction, resilience) that nobody has read as one structure.
This pillar names the fragility in the reader's language before Tony
appears as the answer.

Sub-topics:
1. The 3-question test for payment controls: can the person who creates a new supplier also approve payments to it
2. Why a busy season with zero missed deadlines can just mean the same four people absorbed unpaid evenings
3. The 3-question test for process exceptions that have quietly become the way things are done
4. Why an open-door policy is a queue with one door
5. The bulk fuel distributor with $1.3M delivered but not yet billed, because paper dockets were keyed by one admin
6. The 3-question test for decisions made in group chats that are recorded nowhere else
7. Why every employee being able to open every folder is the absence of a decision about who should

Best formats for this pillar:
→ Pain-point text posts naming the exposure in the reader's own words
→ Numbered carousels walking the 5-risk lens as one system
→ "Are you doing this?" posts that trigger DMs and self-recognition

PILLAR 2, THE ENTERPRISE ARCHITECT GAP
Type: Opinion
─────────────────────────────────────────
Why this pillar: The category claim. Nobody serves the mid-market with a
360-degree structural read across people, process, technology and risk,
chosen on fit and independent of brand. This pillar draws the line between
what a $100M enterprise gets (an architect reading the structure on a
schedule) and what a $20M-$40M firm has only been offered (a vendor, an IT
provider, a single-department consultant). Opinionated by design.

Sub-topics:
1. The enterprise architect gap in one question: who in the building is paid to ask what breaks
2. Why buying a platform because a competitor uses it is borrowing a structure without its reasons
3. The 3-question test for a new system go-live: rollback plan, trigger owner, customer notice
4. Why hiring a senior operator to fix an unmapped structure is an expensive way to find the cracks
5. Why outsourcing a function outsources the doing, not the risk
6. The 3-question test for whether an org chart matches who actually makes decisions
7. The ski resort whose scanners and ticketing disagreed because three systems were never drawn on one page

Best formats for this pillar:
→ Contrarian text posts, plain and unhedged
→ Comparison carousels, $20M-$40M firm vs $100M enterprise on the same structural questions
→ Proof/story posts grounded in real scenarios, translated into a mid-market lesson

PILLAR 3, RESILIENCE OVER GROWTH
Type: Methodology
─────────────────────────────────────────
Why this pillar: Tony's system made concrete. Growth was the only number a
board wanted; recovery time is becoming the other. This pillar carries the
S-Architecture framework (Scale, Secure, Sustain, Shield, Succession) and
THRIVE, translating "resilience" into board-ready numbers a CFO can read.
It pre-sells the Business Resilience Review, Executive Breakthrough
Session, THRIVE and adjacent offers without a hard close. The Fractional
Resilience Advisor retainer gets one soft-sell in the new batch (row 700,
150 rows after its last use at row 550), restoring the ~130+-row distance
standard that row 550 had shortened.

Sub-topics:
1. The 3-question test for a headcount budget that assumes every role is filled for twelve months
2. Why three years of cost-cutting that removed spare capacity is not discipline
3. The 3-question test for forecast variance that is reported but never explained
4. Why a team hitting target every month on overtime has an invoice coming due
5. Why a ten-year-old system that never failed may just be one nobody has watched
6. Why a forty-page policy manual is not preparation and one rehearsed bad day is
7. The 3-question test for a service-level promise against the busiest week of the year

Best formats for this pillar:
→ Step-by-step carousels, the S-Architecture 5-pillar self-score
→ Framework text posts, the recovery-time reframe as a board metric
→ Case/proof posts translated into board language
