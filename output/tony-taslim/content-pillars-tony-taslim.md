PILLAR 1, OPERATIONAL FRAGILITY AS COMMERCIAL RISK
Type: Audience
─────────────────────────────────────────
Why this pillar: The ICP's own world. A COO, CFO or CEO of a founder-led
$10M-$100M firm carries five compounding risks (governance, data integrity,
key-person, friction, resilience) that nobody has read as one structure.
This pillar names the fragility in the reader's language before Tony
appears as the answer.

Sub-topics:
1. A backup nobody has restored is a hope with a schedule: the 3-question restore test
2. The 3-question test for people who have left: how many systems still hold their login
3. The pathology lab whose freezer alarm worked perfectly and texted someone who had left fourteen months earlier
4. The honey packer whose biggest customer's orders sat unseen for four days in a closed shared mailbox
5. Three questions for any dashboard: who owns each number, where the source lives, when it was last reconciled
6. The car park operator whose exceptions tab was always long, so nobody opened it, for fourteen months
7. Why a manual workaround is a loan the business repays in late evenings and errors

Best formats for this pillar:
→ Pain-point text posts naming the exposure in the reader's own words
→ Numbered carousels walking the 5-risk lens as one system
→ "Are you doing this?" posts that trigger DMs and self-recognition

PILLAR 2, THE ENTERPRISE ARCHITECT GAP
Type: Opinion
─────────────────────────────────────────
Why this pillar: The category claim. Nobody serves the mid-market with a
360-degree structural read across people, process, technology and risk,
chosen on fit and independent of brand. This pillar draws the line between
what a $100M enterprise gets (an architect reading the structure on a
schedule) and what a $20M-$40M firm has only been offered (a vendor, an IT
provider, a single-department consultant). Opinionated by design.

Sub-topics:
1. The one-page structure map: circle every line that crosses a department edge
2. The vendor exit test: price doubles or the vendor vanishes, how many weeks to switch
3. Why an IT provider, an accountant and a consultant seeing three slices is not one view of the business
4. The caravan builder whose dealer portal still showed the old chassis rating three months after the supplier changed
5. Why moving to the cloud moves the keys to someone else's building, not the risk
6. The 10-minute order walk: follow one customer order from first email to cash and count who sees all of it
7. The 3-question test for whether your firm has an architect, formal or not

Best formats for this pillar:
→ Contrarian text posts, plain and unhedged
→ Comparison carousels, $20M-$40M firm vs $100M enterprise on the same structural questions
→ Proof/story posts grounded in real scenarios, translated into a mid-market lesson

PILLAR 3, RESILIENCE OVER GROWTH
Type: Methodology
─────────────────────────────────────────
Why this pillar: Tony's system made concrete. Growth was the only number a
board wanted; recovery time is becoming the other. This pillar carries the
S-Architecture framework (Scale, Secure, Sustain, Shield, Succession) and
THRIVE, translating "resilience" into board-ready numbers a CFO can read.
It pre-sells the Business Resilience Review, Executive Breakthrough
Session, THRIVE and adjacent offers without a hard close. The Fractional
Resilience Advisor retainer is rested in the 706-765 batch (last used at
row 700) to hold the ~130-row distance standard.

Sub-topics:
1. The 3-question test for the Succession pillar: is the handover written down, who knows where it lives, has the board read it
2. Why a record revenue quarter is a thinner base if recovery time got longer in the same period
3. The helicopter charter whose booking calendar assumed every aircraft flew every day
4. The thoroughbred stud with seventy percent of its year due in eleven weeks and a cash buffer for six
5. A two-minute S-Architecture self-score: Scale, Secure, Sustain, Shield, Succession, one to five
6. The 3-question test for cash timing: days between paying suppliers and being paid
7. Why lean is a compliment until the first bad week

Best formats for this pillar:
→ Step-by-step carousels, the S-Architecture 5-pillar self-score
→ Framework text posts, the recovery-time reframe as a board metric
→ Case/proof posts translated into board language
